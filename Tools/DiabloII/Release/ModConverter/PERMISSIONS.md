# Permissions: what you may do with the converter and what it makes

This is a plain-language summary of `LICENSE.txt`. If they ever disagree, `LICENSE.txt` wins.

The goal is simple: **more D2R mods that stack in D2RMM.** Use the converter however helps
with that.

## The converter itself (MIT)

You may, without asking:

- **use** it for anything, including converting your own mods or other people's for your own play;
- **modify** it, fork it, port it, or take pieces of it into your own tools;
- **re-upload** it, on Nexus or anywhere else, changed or unchanged, and include it in modding
  toolkits, mod packs or collections;
- **sell** it or use it commercially, as far as the game's and the site's rules allow.

The only condition: copies of the converter itself (or large parts of its code) keep
`LICENSE.txt`, so the copyright and MIT notice stay with it. A link back to the original Nexus page
is welcome but not required.

## Converted mods you make (no conditions from the converter)

The converter puts **no license, credit or notice requirement** on what it writes. The generated
`mod.js`, `mod.json`, patch files and `d2rmm\convert.json` are yours to use, edit, share and
relicense; the code the converter copies into them is covered by the output exception in
`LICENSE.txt`. You don't have to mention the converter (though it's nice if you do).

**What decides whether you may share a converted mod is the original mod's author**, not this
converter. A converted mod still contains that author's work (their tables, strings, art and
plugins):

- **Your own mod:** convert it and release the D2RMM version under any terms you like.
- **Someone else's mod:** fine for your own use. Before uploading it, check the mod page's
  permissions (on Nexus: "Permissions and credits") or ask the author. If they allow it, credit them
  and link to the original.
- **Mod authors:** you are encouraged to ship an official D2RMM version made with the converter next
  to your normal download. The Update button can rebuild it from each new release.
