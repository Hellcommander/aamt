Convert a data mod into a D2RMM mod

A mod that ships whole game files (a <Name>.mpq folder, a .zip / .7z / .rar download or a packed .mpq). The result patches tables and text row by row on top of whatever the other mods did, every feature and every D2RLoader plugin gets a switch in D2RMM's settings, and the mod can be updated later from a new download.

Takes a minute for a big mod; the check at the end runs the result against the game's files.

Update a converted mod from a new download

The old files go to <mod>\d2rmm\previous, the new ones take their place, and the conversion runs again with the settings the mod was converted with. Your switches in D2RMM stay. If the conversion fails, the old files come back.
