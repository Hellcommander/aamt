# Nova Drift-Inspired Mutations - Complete Implementation

## ✅ Added 4 New Experimental Mutations

### 1. Kinetic Blast (Corrupt)
**Inspired by**: Nova Drift Blaster Mutation

**Unique Mechanics**:
- Charges automatically while moving
- Charge rate scales with movement speed
- Fires manually or on head-on collision
- Collision damage scales with crash modifiers
- Launches enemies for chain collision damage

**Balance**:
- Damage: +30%
- Range: -50% (close-range only)
- Fire Rate: -40%
- Accuracy: -25%
- Instability: 12

**Synergy**: Lightweight Barrel + Overclock (charge rate scales)

**Playstyle**: High-risk close-range build. Rewards aggressive movement.

### 2. Sticky Bomb (Corrupt) ✅ BALANCED
**Inspired by**: Nova Drift Grenade Mutation

**Unique Mechanics**:
- Attaches to enemies (doesn't explode immediately)
- Explodes after fuse
- Triggering one explodes all nearby Sticky Bombs
- Stacking increases fuse times
- Chain reactions increase damage and radius
- Higher fire rate but no cluster explosives

**Balance** (Tuned):
- Fire Rate: +15% (was +30%)
- Damage: -15% (sidegrade, not buff)
- Range: -25%
- Accuracy: -20%
- Instability: 10

**Synergy**: Entropy Bloom (triggers shards on explosion)

**Playstyle**: Area control specialist. Rewards stacking bombs on groups.

### 3. Harpoon (Major)
**Inspired by**: Nova Drift Dart Mutation

**Unique Mechanics**:
- Projectiles lodge in targets
- Creates searing hot tether to shooter
- Deals burn damage over time via tether
- Higher impact damage
- Much slower fire rate

**Balance**:
- Damage: +40%
- Fire Rate: -40%
- Range: -20%
- Proc: 100% burn chance

**Synergy**: Burning Rounds (tether burn +50%)

**Playstyle**: DoT-focused build. Rewards sustained fire on targets.

### 4. Greatsword (Corrupt)
**Inspired by**: Nova Drift Swords Mutation

**Unique Mechanics**:
- Deploys one massive sword (not a pair)
- Indestructible
- Damage scales with missing hull (up to +100% at 0% hull)
- Provides armor and damage reduction
- Cannot create Spark Projectiles

**Balance**:
- Base Damage: +50%
- Max Damage: +150% at 0% hull
- Fire Rate: -60%
- Projectile Speed: -50%
- Range: -20%
- Instability: 8

**Synergy**: Condensed Rounds (additional damage scaling)

**Playstyle**: Berserker build. Rewards low-hull aggressive play.
