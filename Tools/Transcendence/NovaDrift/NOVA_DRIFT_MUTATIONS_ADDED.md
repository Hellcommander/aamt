# Nova Drift-Inspired Mutations Added

## Overview
Added 4 new experimental weapon mutations inspired by Nova Drift developer concepts. These mutations fundamentally change weapon behavior, creating entirely new playstyles.

## New Mutations

### 1. Kinetic Blast (Corrupt)
**Concept**: Innately-charging, hard-hitting, close-range weapon

**Mechanics**:
- Charges automatically while moving
- Charge rate scales with movement speed
- Fires manually or on head-on collision
- Collision damage scales with crash damage modifiers
- Launches enemies that can collide with others for chain damage

**Stats**:
- Tier: Corrupt (Slot Cost: 2)
- Damage: +50%
- Range: -40%
- Fire Rate: -30%
- Instability: 12

**Synergies**:
- Lightweight Barrel + Overclock: Charge rate scales with speed bonuses

**Playstyle**: High-risk, high-reward close-range build. Rewards aggressive movement and positioning.

### 2. Sticky Bomb (Corrupt)
**Concept**: Bombs attach to enemies and chain-explode

**Mechanics**:
- Attaches to enemies instead of exploding on impact
- Explodes after short fuse
- Triggering one explodes all nearby Sticky Bombs
- Stacking increases fuse times of attached bombs
- Chain reactions increase damage and radius per reaction

**Stats**:
- Tier: Corrupt (Slot Cost: 2)
- Fire Rate: +30%
- Damage: +25%
- Range: -15%
- Instability: 10

**Synergies**:
- Entropy Bloom: Sticky Bombs trigger shards on explosion

**Playstyle**: Area control and chain reaction specialist. Rewards stacking bombs on groups.

### 3. Harpoon (Major)
**Concept**: Lodges in targets and creates burn tethers

**Mechanics**:
- Projectiles lodge in targets
- Creates searing hot tether to shooter
- Deals burn damage over time via tether
- Higher impact damage than base weapon
- Slower fire rate

**Stats**:
- Tier: Major (Slot Cost: 1)
- Damage: +40%
- Fire Rate: -40%
- Range: -20%
- Proc: 100% burn chance

**Synergies**:
- Burning Rounds: Tether burn damage increased by 50%

**Playstyle**: DoT-focused build. Rewards sustained fire on single targets.

### 4. Greatsword (Corrupt)
**Concept**: Single massive, indestructible sword

**Mechanics**:
- Deploys one massive sword instead of pair
- Indestructible (unlike base Swords)
- Deals more damage the more missing hull you have
- Provides armor and damage reduction
- Cannot create Spark Projectiles

**Stats**:
- Tier: Corrupt (Slot Cost: 2)
- Damage: +80% (scales up to +180% at 0% hull)
- Fire Rate: -50%
- Projectile Speed: -40%
- Instability: 8

**Synergies**:
- Condensed Rounds: Additional damage scaling

**Playstyle**: High-risk berserker build. Rewards low-hull aggressive play.

## Design Philosophy

### Behavior Changes, Not Just Numbers
All new mutations fundamentally change how weapons work:
- **Kinetic Blast**: Movement-based charging, collision mechanics
- **Sticky Bomb**: Attachment and chain reaction system
- **Harpoon**: Tether-based DoT mechanics
- **Greatsword**: Hull-scaling damage, defensive bonuses

### Interesting Tradeoffs
Each mutation has meaningful downsides:
- **Kinetic Blast**: Close-range only, slow fire rate
- **Sticky Bomb**: Reduced range, no cluster explosives
- **Harpoon**: Very slow fire rate, reduced range
- **Greatsword**: Extremely slow, requires low hull for max power

### Synergies
New mutations integrate with existing system:
- Kinetic Blast synergizes with speed mutations
- Sticky Bomb synergizes with Entropy Bloom
- Harpoon synergizes with Burning Rounds
- Greatsword synergizes with Condensed Rounds

## Balance Considerations

### Power Level
- All mutations are sidegrades (change gameplay, not just power)
- Corrupt mutations have high instability
- Tradeoffs are significant and meaningful

### Playstyle Diversity
- **Kinetic Blast**: Aggressive close-range
- **Sticky Bomb**: Area control specialist
- **Harpoon**: DoT sustain build
- **Greatsword**: Berserker high-risk build

### Integration
- Mutations work with existing synergy system
- Conflicts prevent overpowered combinations
- Balance maintained (no dominant combos)
