# Space Whale Ship - Complete AI Visual Description

## Overall Design Philosophy

The Space Whale is a **living biomechanical capital ship** that combines organic whale-like anatomy with advanced technology. It should evoke the feeling of a massive, ancient creature that has been enhanced with crystalline technology, glowing with bioluminescent energy. The visual style draws heavily from **Nova Drift's high-energy aesthetic**: smooth additive glows, procedural distortion effects, particle-driven motion, and layered FX composition.

**Key Visual Principles:**
- **Organic meets Technological**: Smooth, flowing whale-like forms with crystalline tech integration
- **Bioluminescent Energy**: Pulsing, flowing light through internal systems
- **Nova Drift Style**: High-energy glows, additive blending, smooth gradients
- **Massive Scale**: Capital ship proportions (8.0 length × 3.5 width × 2.0 height units)
- **Living Machine**: Should feel alive, breathing, pulsing with energy

## Silhouette & Form

### Primary Shape
The ship follows a **streamlined whale silhouette** with these key characteristics:

- **Head Section**: Bulbous, rounded front with a wide "mouth" opening (drone bay/maw). The head tapers smoothly from a wide front to a narrower connection point. Slightly flattened top-to-bottom, creating a hydrodynamic profile.

- **Mid Section**: Elongated cylindrical body that tapers slightly toward the tail. This is the main body of the ship, housing the Bio-Core and primary systems. The cross-section is oval, wider horizontally than vertically.

- **Belly Bay**: A pronounced ventral bulge in the mid-section, creating a "pregnant" appearance. This houses the drone bay and internal systems. The belly should appear slightly translucent, showing internal bioluminescent activity.

- **Tail Section**: Tapers dramatically from the mid-section to a narrow, flexible tail fin. The tail should appear segmented, allowing for undulating motion. Ends in a wide, horizontal tail fin that can sweep for propulsion.

- **Dorsal Crest**: A prominent fin-like structure running along the top of the ship from head to mid-section. This crest glows with bioluminescent energy and acts as a communication/energy antenna.

### Proportions
- **Length**: 8.0 units (primary dimension)
- **Width**: 3.5 units (at widest point, mid-section)
- **Height**: 2.0 units (excluding dorsal crest)
- **Profile**: Streamlined, hydrodynamic, whale-like

## Materials & Surface Details

### Bioluminescent Skin
The primary surface material is a **living, bioluminescent membrane** with these properties:

- **Base Texture**: Smooth, slightly glossy organic skin with subtle subsurface scattering. The surface should have a slight translucency, allowing internal light to show through.

- **Vein Network**: A complex network of glowing veins runs throughout the ship's surface. These veins pulse with energy, creating flowing patterns of light. The veins are brighter than the base skin and should appear to "flow" with animation.

- **Color Gradient**: The skin transitions from darker, deeper colors at the edges to brighter, more saturated colors along the vein network. The base color is a dark navy blue-gray (#1a2a3a), while veins glow in bright cyan-blue (#66ccff to #88ffff).

- **Carapace Plates**: Crystalline armor plates overlay certain sections (head, dorsal crest, critical systems). These plates are semi-transparent with a crystalline, faceted appearance. They refract and scatter light, creating prismatic effects.

- **Subsurface Scattering**: The skin should show light bleeding through from internal sources, creating a soft, organic glow. This is especially visible in the belly section and along thinner membrane areas.

### Crystalline Technology Integration
Advanced technology appears as **crystalline structures** integrated into the organic form:

- **Crystal Nodes**: Small, faceted crystal formations at key points (joints, energy conduits, weapon mounts). These glow with internal energy and pulse in sync with the ship's systems.

- **Energy Conduits**: Visible energy channels that run along the surface, connecting major systems. These appear as raised, glowing lines that pulse with power.

- **Tech Panels**: Subtle geometric patterns embedded in the skin, suggesting advanced systems beneath. These should be minimal and organic-looking, not harsh or mechanical.

## Bioluminescence & Lighting

### Internal Light Sources
The ship glows from within, creating a **living light** effect:

- **Bio-Core**: A central energy source in the mid-section that pulses rhythmically. This is the brightest light source, visible through the translucent skin. Color: Bright cyan-white (#ffffff).

- **Vein Flow**: Energy flows through the vein network in waves, creating animated light patterns. The flow should appear organic and rhythmic, like blood flow or neural activity.

- **Gill Vents**: Six pairs of gill-like structures along the sides that pulse with light as they "breathe." These create rhythmic light patterns that suggest respiration.

- **Dorsal Crest Glow**: The dorsal fin glows with bioluminescent patterns that can change intensity and color based on the ship's state (idle, active, damaged).

### Lighting Characteristics
- **Intensity**: Emissive intensity of 3.5 (very bright)
- **Pulsing**: All light sources pulse rhythmically at 0.8 speed
- **Flow Speed**: Vein energy flows at 1.2 speed
- **Breathing**: Overall light intensity varies with "breathing" animation (5% amplitude)
