## ARZ tooling lineage

Database build utilities in this ecosystem descend from **arzedit** by Ugnius Soraka (QuasiMod):  
https://gitlab.com/QuasiMod/arzedit  

Configure a working `arzedit` binary via `--arzedit` or `GD_ARZEDIT`. Official packing can also use Crate's Asset Manager.
